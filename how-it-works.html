COMMUNITY RECOMMENDATIONS

Real local experiences

People who have used a listed provider can submit a recommendation about their experience.

Recommendations can include what service was provided, whether they would recommend the provider, whether they would use them again, and comments about their experience.

Recommendations are reviewed before appearing publicly.

Businesses and individuals can be listed

FindLocal757.com is not limited to traditional businesses.

VERIFICATION

What Phone Verified means

Some provider listings may display a Phone Verified badge when the phone number submitted for the listing has been confirmed through FindLocal757.com's phone verification process.

✓ Phone Verified

Phone verification applies only to the submitted phone number. It does not verify identity, business registration, licensing, insurance, qualifications, workmanship, pricing, availability, or future performance.

BEFORE YOU HIRE

FindLocal757.com is a directory, not a guarantee

FindLocal757.com helps organize local provider information and community recommendations. You are still responsible for deciding whether a provider is right for your needs.

Before hiring someone, consider confirming pricing, availability, experience, licensing, insurance, permits, references, and any other information important to the work being performed.

Community recommendations and a Phone Verified badge can provide useful information, but they are not guarantees or endorsements of future performance.
