About NeighborSource

A local directory built by the community, for the community.

Why it exists

Useful local recommendations often disappear inside social media feeds. NeighborSource organizes those recommendations so people can find them later.

The goal is simple: make it easier to find local businesses, side jobs, and individual service providers without forcing people to dig through old posts.

What makes it different

  • Free to browse
  • No pay-to-rank results
  • No lead fees
  • Community recommendations tied to actual services
  • Businesses and individual providers are welcome
