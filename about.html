ABOUT FINDLOCAL757.COM

Local recommendations should be easier to find.

FindLocal757.com brings local businesses, individual service providers, and community recommendations together in one searchable Hampton Roads directory.

WHY IT EXISTS

Finding the person your neighbor recommended should not be difficult.

Hampton Roads already has plenty of local recommendations. The problem is that they are scattered across Facebook groups, neighborhood pages, old posts, text messages, and conversations.

Someone asks for a plumber, dog sitter, mechanic, handyman, photographer, or notary. Neighbors share names they trust. A few weeks later, someone else asks the same question and everyone starts over.

FindLocal757.com is designed to organize that information into one place that is easier to search, browse, and use.

WHAT FINDLOCAL757.COM DOES

A simple local service directory

The goal is to make finding local help easier without turning the process into another complicated marketplace.

🔎

Organizes Providers

Businesses and individual service providers are organized by category, service, and location.
👍

Tracks Recommendations

Community recommendations can be connected directly to provider listings.
📍

Keeps It Local

🛠️

Helps When You're Stuck

If you cannot find the right provider, you can tell the community what kind of help you need.

WHAT FINDLOCAL757.COM IS NOT

Not another pay-to-win service marketplace

FindLocal757.com is focused on organizing local service information, not selling access to customers or controlling the relationship between providers and the people who hire them.

✓ No paid provider rankings
✓ No lead fees for contacting providers
✓ No account required just to browse
✓ No requirement to book through FindLocal757.com
✓ Providers and customers communicate directly
✓ Free to search and currently free to list

CHOOSING A PROVIDER

You remain in control of who you hire

FindLocal757.com is a community directory and information resource. It does not recommend, endorse, employ, approve, or guarantee any business or individual listed on the site.

Provider listings and community recommendations are provided for informational purposes so users can discover local service providers, contact them directly, and decide for themselves whether they want to work with them.

FindLocal757.com does not independently verify a provider's identity, business registration, licensing, insurance, permits, qualifications, experience, workmanship, or suitability for a particular job.

Before hiring or paying someone, independently confirm whatever matters for your job, including pricing, availability, experience, references, licensing, insurance, permits, qualifications, business status, and other relevant details.
