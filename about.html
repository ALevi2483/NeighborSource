ABOUT NEIGHBORSOURCE

Local recommendations should be easier to find.

NeighborSource organizes local businesses and individual service providers into one searchable community directory.

WHY NEIGHBORSOURCE EXISTS

Finding the person your neighbor recommended should not be difficult.

Most communities already have plenty of recommendations. The problem is that they are scattered across Facebook groups, neighborhood pages, old posts, text messages, and conversations.

Someone asks for a plumber, dog sitter, mechanic, handyman, photographer, or notary. Neighbors respond with names they trust. A few weeks later, someone else asks the same question and the process starts over again.

NeighborSource is designed to organize those recommendations into something easier to search and use.

WHAT NEIGHBORSOURCE DOES

A simple local service directory

The goal is to make finding local help easier without creating another complicated marketplace.

🔎

Organizes Providers

Businesses and individual service providers are organized by category, service, and location.
👍

Tracks Recommendations

Community recommendations can be connected directly to provider listings.
📍

Keeps It Local

Listings show the communities and service areas where providers work.
🛠️

Helps When You Are Stuck

If you cannot find the right provider, you can submit what you need instead.
BUILT AROUND THE COMMUNITY

NeighborSource becomes more useful when people share the providers they actually use.

1

Someone Finds a Good Provider

A neighbor has a positive experience with a local business or individual service provider.

2

The provider can be suggested for the directory or recommended if already listed.

3

The Next Neighbor Can Find Them

Instead of asking the same question again, another neighbor can search NeighborSource.

WHAT NEIGHBORSOURCE IS NOT

Not another pay-to-win service marketplace

NeighborSource is designed to remain focused on organizing local information rather than selling access to customers.

✓ No paid provider rankings
✓ No lead fees for contacting a provider
✓ No account required just to browse
✓ No requirement to book through NeighborSource
✓ Providers and customers communicate directly

VERIFICATION

Verification is specific, not a blanket endorsement

Some listings may include verification badges when certain information has been reviewed.

✓ Phone Verified ✓ Business Verified ✓ License Verified ✓ Insurance Verified

A badge only means that the specific information represented by that badge was reviewed. It does not guarantee pricing, workmanship, availability, reliability, or future performance.

Learn About Verification
CHOOSING A PROVIDER

You remain in control of who you hire

NeighborSource provides information to help people find local service providers. It does not hire providers, negotiate pricing, process payments, supervise work, or guarantee results.

Before hiring someone, users should confirm whatever information matters for the job, including pricing, licensing, insurance, references, permits, qualifications, and availability when applicable.
