Privacy Policy

Plain-language starter policy for the prototype site.

1. Information We Collect

NeighborSource may collect information you voluntarily submit through listing, recommendation, request, report, and contact forms.

2. How We Use Information

Information is used to operate the directory, review submissions, publish approved listings and recommendations, respond to requests, and improve the site.

3. Public Information

Approved provider information may be displayed publicly. Personal contact information submitted only for moderation or verification should not be published unless clearly identified for public display.

4. Third-Party Services

The final site may use free services such as GitHub Pages, Google Forms, and Google Sheets. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

5. Contact

Use the Contact page to request corrections or removal of submitted information.

This is starter language for the prototype and is not legal advice. Review it before publishing the final site.
