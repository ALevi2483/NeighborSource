Privacy Policy

NeighborSource is designed to collect only the information needed to operate and maintain the local service directory.

Last updated: October 26, 2026

1. Information We Collect

NeighborSource may collect information that you voluntarily provide when using forms on the website.

Depending on the form you use, this information may include:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • City or service area
  • Business or service-provider information
  • Service descriptions
  • Website or Facebook page information
  • Community recommendations and comments
  • Service requests
  • Listing update requests
  • Reports about listings
  • Messages submitted through the contact form

2. How We Use Information

Information submitted to NeighborSource may be used to:

  • Create and maintain provider listings
  • Review submitted businesses and individual service providers
  • Review community recommendations
  • Respond to service requests
  • Process listing updates or removal requests
  • Review reports about listings
  • Respond to questions and feedback
  • Maintain the accuracy and usefulness of the directory
  • Prevent misuse, spam, or fraudulent submissions

3. Information That May Be Public

NeighborSource is a public service directory. Information intended for a provider listing may be displayed publicly after the listing is reviewed and approved.

Public provider information may include:

  • Provider or business name
  • Provider type
  • Service category
  • Services offered
  • Provider description
  • Business phone number
  • Website
  • Facebook page
  • Service area
  • Applicable verification badges
  • Approved community recommendation information
Do not submit information for public display that you do not want shared publicly.

4. Information We Do Not Intentionally Display

Certain information is collected for administration, moderation, or follow-up and is not intended to be displayed publicly.

This may include:

  • Provider contact-person names when they are different from the public listing name
  • Provider administrative email addresses
  • Recommendation submitter email addresses
  • Reporter email addresses
  • Contact-form email addresses
  • Internal moderation notes

5. Community Recommendations

Recommendations submitted about providers may be reviewed before being displayed publicly.

Approved recommendations may display information such as the reviewer's name, city, service received, recommendation response, whether the reviewer would use the provider again, and submitted comments.

Recommendation email addresses are collected for moderation or follow-up and are not intended for public display.

6. Service Requests

Information submitted through the "I Need Someone" form is used to understand the service being requested and help manage community service requests.

Users should avoid including sensitive personal information in service descriptions.

7. How Information Is Stored

NeighborSource currently uses Google Sheets and Google Apps Script to receive and manage website submissions.

The public website is hosted using GitHub Pages.

These third-party services may process technical information as part of operating their platforms according to their own privacy policies and security practices.

8. Selling Personal Information

NeighborSource does not sell personal information submitted through the website.

NeighborSource is not designed as a lead-selling service and does not charge providers for individual customer contact information.

9. Advertising and Paid Rankings

NeighborSource does not currently use paid provider rankings.

Providers cannot pay to appear above other providers simply because they purchased a higher placement.

10. Cookies and Technical Information

NeighborSource does not currently operate its own advertising or behavioral-tracking system.

However, services used to host or operate NeighborSource, including GitHub and Google services, may automatically collect technical information such as IP addresses, browser information, device information, or access logs as part of their normal operations.

11. Links to Other Websites

Provider listings may include links to websites, social-media pages, or other third-party services.

NeighborSource does not control the privacy practices, security, or content of third-party websites.

Users should review the privacy policies of those services before submitting personal information to them.

12. Sensitive Information

Please do not submit sensitive personal information through NeighborSource unless it is specifically necessary for the requested service.

This includes information such as:

  • Social Security numbers
  • Financial account information
  • Credit or debit card numbers
  • Passwords
  • Government identification numbers
  • Detailed medical information
  • Other highly sensitive personal information

13. Accuracy and Removal Requests

If information about you or your business is inaccurate, you may submit a request through the Update a Listing page.

You may also request removal of a listing through the same process.

Update or remove a listing →

14. Reports and Moderation

NeighborSource may review, edit, reject, restrict, or remove submitted content when necessary to maintain the accuracy, safety, usefulness, or integrity of the directory.

Report a listing →

15. Data Retention

NeighborSource may retain submissions and administrative records for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the directory, review disputes, maintain records, prevent abuse, or respond to requests.

Information that is no longer needed may be deleted or removed from active records.

16. Security

Reasonable efforts are made to limit access to administrative information and protect information submitted through NeighborSource.

However, no website, online service, or electronic storage system can guarantee complete security.

17. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as NeighborSource changes, adds features, or changes the services used to operate the website.

The updated date at the top of this page will be changed when material revisions are made.

18. Questions About Privacy

Questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy can be submitted through the NeighborSource contact page.
