FindLocal757.com Phone Verification

Phone verification helps show when a provider's submitted phone number has been confirmed through our verification process.

WHAT PHONE VERIFICATION MEANS

Phone Verified means the submitted phone number has been confirmed

FindLocal757.com may display a Phone Verified badge when the phone number submitted for a provider listing has been confirmed through our phone verification process.

Phone verification applies only to the phone number associated with the listing.

Important: A Phone Verified badge is not an endorsement, background check, license verification, insurance verification, or guarantee of the provider.

WHAT PHONE VERIFICATION DOES NOT MEAN

A Phone Verified badge is not a guarantee

✕ It does not verify the provider's identity.
✕ It does not verify that a business is registered or legally formed.
✕ It does not verify licenses, certifications, or permits.
✕ It does not verify insurance coverage.
✕ It does not verify qualifications or experience.
✕ It does not guarantee workmanship or quality.
✕ It does not guarantee pricing or estimates.
✕ It does not guarantee availability or future performance.
✕ It does not replace your own research before hiring someone.
✕ It does not mean FindLocal757.com recommends or endorses the provider.
NO PHONE VERIFIED BADGE?

Lack of a badge does not automatically mean something is wrong

Some providers may not have completed FindLocal757.com's phone verification process.

A provider without a Phone Verified badge may still be a legitimate and capable local service provider. The absence of the badge simply means FindLocal757.com is not displaying a Phone Verified status for that listing.
