Verification & Safety

Know what each NeighborSource badge means before you hire someone.

Verification Badges

✓ Phone Verified Confirms a working phone number.

✓ Email Verified Confirms a valid email address.

✓ Identity Verified Confirms the submitted identity information.

✓ Business Verified Confirms the listed business information.

✓ License Verified Confirms a license when required and provided.

✓ Insurance Verified Confirms proof of insurance when provided.

Important

Verification confirms specific information submitted to NeighborSource. It does not guarantee quality, safety, licensing requirements, insurance coverage, or the outcome of any work.

Before hiring

  • Confirm required licenses for your job.
  • Ask for current insurance when appropriate.
  • Get written pricing and scope for larger jobs.
  • Do not send unusual upfront payments to unknown providers.
