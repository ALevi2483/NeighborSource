FindLocal757.com Verification

Verification badges show when specific information associated with a provider listing has been reviewed.

WHAT VERIFICATION MEANS

Verification applies only to the item shown

FindLocal757.com may review certain information associated with a provider and display a badge when that specific information has been verified.

For example, a provider with a Phone Verified badge has had their submitted phone information reviewed. It does not automatically mean their business, license, insurance, or other information has also been verified.

Important: Verification is not the same as an endorsement or guarantee.
VERIFICATION BADGES

What each badge means

Only badges actually displayed on a provider's profile should be considered verified.

✓ Phone Verified

Phone Verified

The provider's submitted phone number has been reviewed or confirmed through the FindLocal757.com verification process.

This does not confirm the provider's identity, business status, licensing, insurance, or quality of work.

✓ Business Verified

Business Verified

Information supporting the existence or identity of the listed business has been reviewed.

This does not mean every statement made by the business has been independently verified.

✓ License Verified

License Verified

License information associated with the provider has been reviewed when a license is applicable to the service.

Licensing requirements vary by service and jurisdiction. Users should still confirm current license status when it matters for the work.

✓ Insurance Verified

Insurance Verified

Insurance information provided for the listing has been reviewed.

Coverage can change, expire, contain exclusions, or apply differently depending on the work being performed. Confirm current coverage directly when needed.

WHAT VERIFICATION DOES NOT MEAN

A badge is not a guarantee

Verification gives you an additional piece of information about a provider. It should not replace your own judgment or research.

✕ It does not guarantee workmanship.
✕ It does not guarantee pricing or estimates.
✕ It does not guarantee availability.
✕ It does not guarantee future performance.
✕ It does not replace your own research.
✕ It does not mean FindLocal757.com endorses the provider.
NO VERIFICATION BADGE?

No badge does not automatically mean something is wrong

Some providers may not have completed any FindLocal757.com verification steps.

A provider without verification badges may still be a legitimate and capable local service provider. The absence of a badge simply means FindLocal757.com is not displaying that specific verification.
