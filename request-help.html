BEFORE HIRING

Use good judgment when choosing a provider

NeighborSource helps organize local service information and community recommendations. It does not guarantee a provider's work, pricing, availability, licensing, insurance, or qualifications.

Confirm important details directly with the provider before agreeing to work or making payment.
