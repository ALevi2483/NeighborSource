RECOMMENDATION GUIDELINES

Keep it useful and fair

Recommendations should describe your own experience with the provider. Avoid rumors, personal attacks, private information, or claims about situations you did not personally experience.

NeighborSource may decline or remove submissions that are abusive, misleading, unrelated, or cannot reasonably be connected to the provider.
