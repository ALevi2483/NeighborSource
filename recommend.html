RECOMMENDATION GUIDELINES

Keep it useful and fair

Recommendations should describe your own experience with the provider. Focus on the service you received, communication, reliability, and other details you personally observed.

Avoid rumors, personal attacks, private information, or claims about situations you did not personally experience.

FindLocal757.com may decline, edit for formatting or clarity, or remove submissions that are abusive, misleading, unrelated, or cannot reasonably be connected to the provider.
